Email Engagement Call List

Who is engaging with our email, ranked most engaged to least. 1 point per open, 3 per click, 5 per reply. No cap, no time decay. Window: 2026-06-01 to 2026-08-29. Generated 2026-08-31 15:40 ET.

Automation email only. Opens, clicks and replies count only on threads sent by one of the 14 running automations. Estimates, project conversations, and other staff members' threads are excluded, and where a contact has that kind of activity it is named on the row but not scored. A reply to a drip counts, including when it comes back as "Re: " on the same thread. Everyone is scored over the same window, June 1 forward, so a contact added in August is not competing against one you have had since 2023. One gap that cannot be closed: Pipedrive attaches only open events to a person record, so clicks exist nowhere but the notification feed, and that feed holds 30 days. Clicks before roughly July 19 are gone for good, so click-driven scores are understated for the first seven weeks of the window. Running this on a schedule is what stops that loss going forward. Nobody is ever removed: people stay on after they drop out of the live feed. Hover a Clicks, Opens or Replies number to see the individual events behind it, with the email each one belongs to.

Score = 1 point per open, 3 per click, 5 per reply, uncapped and never decayed, so it is a lifetime total rather than a recency reading. That is what makes the Last column matter when you pick who to call: a 40 earned in June and a 40 earned this week read the same here. Tiers: HOT 50 and up, WARM 25 and up, COOL 10 and up. Names, companies and contact details are removed in this shared view. Shaded rows are a mail security scanner rather than a person: the SCANNER tier is someone whose every tracked event fired in a machine burst, and the flag on a tiered row means part of their activity looks automated. Hover the flag for the reason.

Turning this into a Pipedrive view

Pipedrive has no native field for opens or clicks, so a saved list view needs the tier written onto the person record first. The path is: create one person field called Email Engagement Tier, set it on the tiered records, then save a Contacts filter on that field. Nothing has been written to Pipedrive yet.

The ID lists that drive that filter are in the internal copy.

Coverage

403 distinct recipients appear in the 30-day feed. 342 are treated as real people; 61 are listed separately because every single tracked event on them fired in a machine burst alongside five or more other recipients within minutes.

Every one of those 342 real contacts was looked up in Pipedrive. Only 3 came back with no record at all. The scanner entries were not looked up, since they are not people.

233 people are marked banked: they have already dropped out of Pipedrive's live feed but are kept here from an earlier scan. Their numbers are frozen at whatever the last scan saw. That count only grows, and it is the whole reason the ledger exists.

3 rows carry an opens only warning: their whole score is opens with not one click. With opens now worth a point each and uncapped, a mail client that re-fetches the tracking pixel can push someone to the top of this list without a human ever reading anything. Treat those scores as soft until they click something.

173 contacts have a Pipedrive record but no usable phone number, so they fall back to an email button. That, not engagement, is the ceiling on how long the call list can get.
